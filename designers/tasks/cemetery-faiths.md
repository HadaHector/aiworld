# Task: faiths for the cemetery

Read `designers/BRIEF.md` first, then this.

## Already decided (do not pitch these again)

The cemetery itself is agreed; `designers/cemetery-chapel.md` has it. In short: a walled yard on a
rise, out of the settlements, one track to it, the chapel at its top, graves in rows by age, a
gateway, a plague mound outside, a bone house, a keeper's hut, conditions from kept to ruined,
one light at night, grave robbers later.

## What we want now

This is a fantasy world, so its faiths are its own. Today every chapel is the same building with
the same cross. We want **culture variants**: the same cemetery rule, dressed by different faiths,
so two cemeteries in different lands look like they were made by different peoples.

**Pitch exactly 3 faiths.** For each:

- **Name and belief**: what they believe about death, in two sentences, and only the parts
  that show in what they build. No theology that cannot be seen.
- **Grave markers**: their shapes, sizes in metres, materials, how they wear. How a rich grave
  differs from a poor one.
- **The chapel**: its plan, roof, tower or none, doors, windows, what is inside that matters,
  all as a variation of a house generator (blocks, roofs, openings), not a one-off model.
- **Symbols**: the 1–3 shapes they cut, paint or hang. Simple enough to be a few triangles or a
  painted mark, readable at 20 m.
- **Special features**: what this faith adds or removes from the agreed cemetery (no wall, a
  different layout, offerings, a burning place, something in the trees...), and how it reads at
  night.
- **Where it lives**: which climates and voices (verdant, arid, frozen, murky, stony, shaded) or
  biomes, and how it sits next to the others.
- **From afar**: the one silhouette or colour that tells a walker this faith from a kilometre.

## Your lens

Five designers each pitch three, so stay in your own lens, and avoid the first idea anyone would
have: no sun temple, no skull-covered death god, no druid stone circle, no sacred tree with a face,
and no copy of a real religion with its name changed. Borrow from real practice, but mix and bend
it.

- **Marta**: faiths of ancestors and memory, how the dead are kept among the living.
- **Rafael**: faiths shaped by how a people make their living, and what death costs them.
- **Saga**: faiths of hard places and fear, what they protect themselves from.
- **Doug**: faiths read in the land: stone, water, height, the shape of the ground.
- **Priya**: faiths told apart without words: by silhouette, colour, light and pattern.

## Format

Short. One block per faith with the headings above, no full pitch format. Then **The one I'd
build first**, and why, in two sentences. No "What I'd cut" this time.
