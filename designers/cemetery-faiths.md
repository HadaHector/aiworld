# Cemetery faiths: the panel's pitches, merged

Task: `designers/tasks/cemetery-faiths.md`. 15 faiths from 5 designers (default effort, each a
lens, 2026-10-12). They collapse into 8 distinct ideas: 3 clusters and 5 one-offs.

## Clusters (several designers, same core)

### Water and boats: Marta, Priya, Rafael, Saga, Doug
The dead go out on the water.
- **Boat-hull roof** on the chapel: a ridge as keel, rising at both ends (Priya, Rafael). One new
  roof shape in the house generator.
- **Markers**:
  - upturned hulls on stone blocks, keel up, tarred black (Marta);
  - boat-shaped slabs with a lifted prow (Priya);
  - tall posts cut like a bow stem, with a colour band at the foot; a post with no mound for those
    lost at sea (Rafael);
  - tall leaning notched posts, a notch per household death (Saga).
- **Layout**: no wall, the water is the boundary.
  - Graves on a pier or causeway into the reeds (Priya).
  - A post row along a shingle spit "like a fleet at anchor" (Rafael).
  - A dredged island reached by a corduroy track (Saga).
  - A chapel on piers with a stream running under the altar, entered by a footbridge (Doug).
- **Night**: one lamp per fresh grave at the end of the pier (Priya). A lantern on the bridge or
  the highest post, doubled by its reflection (Saga, Doug).
- **Symbols**: a paddle; a line with a dot; a float mark (a ring with a bar); wavy lines.
- **From afar**: a black, sharp-ended roof dipping in the middle, seen across flat water.

### Cairns that grow: Marta, Doug, Rafael
A grave is added to: a stone per visit or per family that passes.
- **Markers**:
  - from 0.4 m when new to 2.5 m when old;
  - a quartz ring at the base for the rich (Marta);
  - a notched slab on top (Doug);
  - a sheep bell on a pole (Rafael).
- **Meaning**: a slumped cairn is forgotten; a cairn with its top taken is robbed.
- **Chapel**:
  - none: an open roof on four posts over a stone table (Marta);
  - an unroofed drystone tower with a beacon, lit at a death (Doug);
  - a turf fold-house whose hearth is never let out, smoke rising from its roof (Rafael).
- **Layout**:
  - rings round the tower, oldest at the centre (Doug);
  - a line of cairns along a through drove road, the one cemetery you meet on the way (Rafael).
- **Cost**: the heap generator reused, so cheap.

### Lamps in the graves: Saga, Priya
Each gravestone has a niche with a lamp, because the dead need light to find the way.
- **Breaks the agreed "one light" rule on purpose**: the whole yard glows. Lamps are emissive
  sprites, not real lights.
- **What the light tells you**: a ruined yard goes dark; a robbed niche is a dark gap in a lit
  yard.
- **Chapel**:
  - a square lantern-house with a tall window on each face (Saga);
  - a 12 m tower with a lit lantern crown and a black band, beside a low flat-roofed nave
    (Priya).
- **Symbols**: a circled dot; a flame of three triangles.
- **Where**: arid and stony country with clear skies.

  pyramid), and the heap generator for cairns.
